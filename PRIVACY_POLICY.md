# Privacy Policy for CYBERLOOP 2077 

**Game:** CYBERLOOP 2077  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-10  
**Contact:** pudingearl@gmail.com
---

### What we collect
When a run ends, the game sends the following data to our server for the leaderboards:

- A **random anonymous player ID** (generated on your device; it is not linked to your name, email, account or device identifiers)
- Run statistics: kills, run time, total score, accuracy, hits taken, stages passed, and whether you used "continue" (rewarded ad)

We do **not** ask for or store your name, email, location or contacts. We do **not** store your IP address or browser/device details in our database.

### Why we use it
- To show the leaderboards (top scores and in-game run times).
- To keep the game working reliably (e.g. detecting impossible scores / abuse and rate-limiting requests).
- To understand, in aggregate and statistically, what players enjoy, so we can improve the game.

We do not sell this data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

### Storage and retention
- Your best score and best run time are stored under the anonymous ID on our server and database provider (render.com, mongodb.com).
- Technical request logs (anonymous ID, request type, time) are deleted automatically after 3 days. Aggregated request counters contain no personal data.
- Leaderboards never show your ID; other players only see ranks, scores and times.

### Ads and platforms
Ads and platform services are provided by third parties (e.g. [CRAZYGAMES / PLAYGAMA / OTHER]). They have their own privacy policies and may collect data independently of us.

### Your choices
- Deleting the game's local data creates a new anonymous ID on your next run (your old entry becomes unreachable for you).
- To request deletion of your server-side data, contact pudingearl@gmail.com and include your player ID if you can find it.

### Children
Children's Privacy Our game does not knowingly collect personal, identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

### Changes
We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
